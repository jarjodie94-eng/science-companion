📖 Lesson 1 — Observation or Interpretation?

Scientists often begin by observing what they can directly see, hear, measure or detect.

An observation describes what is noticed. An interpretation explains what you think that observation means.


🧠 Try this

You see water droplets on the outside of a cold glass.

📏 Lesson 2 — Qualitative or Quantitative?

Scientists can record observations in different ways.

Qualitative observations describe qualities or characteristics.

Quantitative observations use numbers or measurements.


🧠 Try this

“The liquid is 24°C.”
