# Unit 00 – Preparing for Study

## Welcome

Welcome to your first Open University science unit.

This unit is designed to help you prepare for university-level study and develop good study habits before beginning the course.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this unit you should be able to:

- Understand how your module is organised.
- Create a realistic study routine.
- Take useful science notes.
- Feel confident about starting your studies.

---

## Key Tip

You don't need to know everything before you start.

The goal is to build consistent study habits and enjoy learning.

---

## Remember

Every expert scientist started as a beginner.
