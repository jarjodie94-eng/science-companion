📖 Study Tip 1 — Small Sessions Count

Studying does not have to mean sitting at a desk for several hours.

A focused 20–30 minute session can be useful, especially when followed by a short break.

The important thing is returning regularly rather than trying to do everything in one session.

📝 Study Tip 2 — Active Notes

Try not to copy everything word-for-word.

Instead, ask yourself:

  • 💡 What is the main idea?
  • ❓ What do I not understand yet?
  • 🔑 Which words or ideas are important?
  • 🧪 Can I explain this in my own words?

🧠 Study Tip 3 — Active Recall

One useful way to revise is to try to remember something before looking at the answer.

That is why your Science Companion includes flashcards and quizzes.
