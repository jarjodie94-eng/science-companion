❓ What is science?

Science is a systematic way of investigating and understanding the natural world using evidence, observation and testing.


❓ Why is evidence important in science?

Evidence allows ideas and explanations to be tested and evaluated rather than relying only on opinion or assumption.


❓ Why are active notes useful?

Active notes help you process information in your own words, identify important ideas and make later revision easier.


❓ What is active recall?

Active recall means trying to retrieve information from memory before checking the answer.


🔬 S111 – Unit 01

Science and Observation

👀 Observation vs Interpretation

Question: What is the difference between an observation and an interpretation?

✨ Reveal Answer

Observation: something you directly notice, detect or measure.

Interpretation: an explanation of what you think the observation means.

🔢 Qualitative vs Quantitative

Question: What is the difference between qualitative and quantitative observations?

✨ Reveal Answer

Qualitative: describes qualities or characteristics.

Quantitative: uses numbers or measurements.

🔎 Careful Scientific Observations

Question: What makes a good scientific observation?

✨ Reveal Answer

A good scientific observation is clear, specific and accurately recorded.

Where possible, scientists use measurements and precise details rather than vague descriptions.
